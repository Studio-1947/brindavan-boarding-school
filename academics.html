STAGE BY STAGE

Three stages.
One continuous journey.

Each stage builds on the last, so that skills learned early carry through to the ISC years.

01THE FOUNDATIONS

Primary years

Reading, expression, numeracy and an early introduction to science, social studies and the arts. The focus is on curiosity, confident communication and building study habits.

Discuss entry-level admission
02GROWING IN CONFIDENCE

Middle & secondary

Subject teaching deepens across languages, mathematics, the sciences, social studies and computer applications, alongside structured revision and assessment as students approach the ICSE examinations.

Ask about classes & subjects
03THE NEXT CHAPTER

Senior secondary

Students choose subject combinations for the ISC pathway that align with their interests: sciences, commerce or humanities, with more independent, exam-focused study.

Enquire about ISC options

HOW LEARNING HAPPENS

Classroom
teaching &
assessment.

Structured teaching, regular assessment, steady progress.

Classes follow a timetabled structure across core subjects, with periodic tests and terminal examinations used to track progress ahead of the board examinations. Teachers work with students individually where extra support or stretch is needed.

Homework, class tests and project work are used throughout the year, so that the ICSE and ISC examinations are the culmination of ongoing assessment rather than a single high-stakes moment.
